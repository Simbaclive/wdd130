Featured Expeditions

Thundering Canyon Run

Experience the ultimate adrenaline rush on our most popular high-intensity route. Tackle class IV and V rapids surrounded by breathtaking canyon walls. Perfect for thrill-seekers looking for an unforgettable challenge.

Family Scenic Float

A relaxed, fun-filled journey down gentle class I and II rapids. Enjoy stunning local wildlife, clear waters, and expert guides who make this a safe and memorable experience for all ages.

Two-Day Wilderness Expedition

Immerse yourself fully in nature. This overnight camping and rafting trip takes you deep into remote river territories with riverside camp meals and starlit skies.
